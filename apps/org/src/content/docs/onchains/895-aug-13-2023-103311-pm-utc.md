First things first... HAPPY BIRTHDAY X7 FINANCE!

I want to take this opportunity to say thank each and every one of you for creating, managing and developing probably the most fascinating community I have seen in all my years in the space. The efforts from everyone is truly humbling and it makes me proud to watch what has unfolded in the past 12 months.

From our side, it's been a mix of emotions. Providing the vision of X7 Finance was the easy part, implementing this at every stage not so much. I am happy that the "finish line" is almost in sight. This will give us some time to reflect on what we have built and with the help of you, the people who will ultimately control the project, the future of X7 Finance is completely limitless.

In my opinion, the greatest thing about this project is the fact it will not rely on a single dev/team for the entire future of the project.

During the not so recent AMA with one of my colleagues, a question was asked to the 7 developers of X7 Finance.

Q. What is your end goal in crypto?

A. Although a very short question, this is difficult to answer but the truth is I have no single end goal. Decentralized Finance to me is an escape from being controlled by the rich. I would be lying if I said "It's not about the money", of course it is but making a difference in the space would be my reserved answer. This is rarely done, especially in the last few years. The majority of the space has moved on from smart developers building useful utilities, instead it's cash grabs and scams. This is something that I believe needs to be overshadowed by good tech. Initial liquidity loans may not be seen by some as "good tech" but it's something that is badly required within the space. Some of the smartest people I have came across in life have came from poor geographical areas and without the capital required to start up, they hit a road block. This is one of the reasons why X7 Finance can help, within crypto and beyond.

What is your end goal in crypto?

While I don't have a singular end goal in crypto, I am driven by a set of core principles and beliefs.
These principles have the gravity to reshape our world for the collective benefit.

Central to my philosophy is the belief that libertarian values pave the way for true egalitarianism.

- We should champion the freedom of creation.
- Everyone should have equal access to the tools of creation.
- Opportunity should be equally distributed.
- Every individual should have the autonomy to lead their life according to their vision.

With X7, my aspiration is to be the premier liquidity provider across all blockchains,
ensuring that everyone has the ability to create and seize equal opportunities.

The blockchain offers a canvas where any idea can come to life. Within our community, pioneers and
token holders contribute to these innovative creations, and our entire ecosystem flourishes from
these endeavors.

These principles deeply influence my approach to product and software design.

My philosophy emphasizes that the utility of our products and platforms should cater to the broadest
possible audience. Our journey has been made possible by our pioneers and token holders. As our
revenue grows, my commitment remains to maximize the value and rewards for our token holders.

As a Computer Scientist and Economist, I see finance mediated through cryptographic and networked
software as the epitome of freedom. The joy in translating these ideas into tangible products has
been immensely fulfilling and every challenge faced and every milestone achieved adds to my
repository of wisdom and knowledge.

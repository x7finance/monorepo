Long journey isn't it?

From the highs to the lows to the Rick Roll days ( We were not involved in the original Rick Roll 😂 )

The community formed here has something incredible and I highly doubt it can be repeated elsewhere.
We appreciate all your support. Trust me when I say none of us take it for granted and continue to push towards new chapters of X7 growth and development.

I have been in this industry for a while now.
I've seen a lot of great ideas getting adopted and others getting trashed. I've seen a lot of changes good and bad. I've seen a lot of people come and leave. Unfortunately, It's not easy to tell when a project will be successful, and it's far too difficult to foresee if your efforts are in vane or not.

I believe money is important (aren't we are all apes inside). At the same time, no day goes by without thinking about what can I do better. How can I improve this space? What can I do for the people? How can I make a lasting impression in this space and be _the_ dev?
Well, the answers to that are too many.

It's clear to everyone's eyes that the economic system is far from perfect. Too many hands micromanage every cent. The powers that be effectively have access to your finances and your investment decisions.

Creativity and innovation may not flourish in an environment like this. If we continue to stand idly then the outcome will always be the same.
What I observe here is a huge chance of change. I would call this the “people's revenge”. Crypto gives us a huge opportunity to entirely reshape the economic systems while leaving the bad apples that shaped the past.

We have the opportunity to make it right. To give equal chances to anyone on this planet with just a phone and internet access to bring to life their wildest ideas. We can do this without any intermediary dictating what you're allowed to do. It should be about what you can do.

The code will do this for us. The code doesn't have eyes to judge. The code will not be an unfair middleman. I will not be done until we see it thru.

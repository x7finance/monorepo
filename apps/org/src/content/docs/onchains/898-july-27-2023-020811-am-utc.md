Q: What is your end goal in crypto?

A: If you asked me this question a handful of years ago, you probably would have received this as an answer https://youtube.com/shorts/y55XrE1ghfk?feature=share

But on a serious note… we still find ourselves in the elementary stages of the tech. It's certainly easy to get wrapped up in the fun. In many ways it should be fun. Although there is still lots of work to be done. We must not stop at apes and penguins. Dogs & frogs. Maximum yields. Wen moon?

Educating the masses on crypto and blockchain should not end at financial literacy.
Blockchain technologies may allow us to live in a world where law and structure may be enshrined on chain.
Where truth and validity may be verified by consensus.  
Identity provable yet private & immutable.
Someday history not rewritten by the victor or the highest bidder - but by a simple search within the ledger.

If the general public is not privy to these emerging systems that will move societies, we may fall in love with a worse form than what we have now at the very beginning.

I believe crypto is freedom. We first must make it so. I hope to contribute towards this mission. 🫡

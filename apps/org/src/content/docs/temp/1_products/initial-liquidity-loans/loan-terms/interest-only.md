# Loan: Interest Only

## Advantage

With this option, you make a small upfront payment and then continue with premium payments each period. The principal loan amount doesn't reduce over time, making this an excellent choice for those who prefer to keep their cash flow fluid.

### Beneficiary

Ideal for ventures that need to preserve capital for growth and can meet the regular interest payments.

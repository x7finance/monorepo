# Loan: Amortizing

## Advantage

This loan type is all about balance. It requires a small upfront cost, with premium and principal payments made over each defined period. The gradual repayment structure eases the financial burden and allows for sustainable growth.

### Beneficiary

Best suited for projects that anticipate steady revenue streams and prefer to distribute their repayment over time.

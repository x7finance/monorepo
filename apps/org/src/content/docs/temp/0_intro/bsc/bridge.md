# 2. Getting BNB on BSC

The native token on Binance Smart Chain network is BEP20 BNB.

In order to complete most basic transactions on the network, you will need to pay gas. Gas fees are paid in BEP20 BNB.

There are various ways in which you can acquire BEP20 BNB.

+++ 1. Metamask / Trust Wallet

You may purchase various cryptocurrencies within your Metamask wallet via third party's.

<https://metamask.io/buy-crypto/>

Trust Wallet also supports similar functionality and you may find guidance in the link below

<https://blog.trustwallet.com/blog/how-to-add-the-arbitrum-token-to-trust-wallet>

+++ 2. CEXs

Additionally you can opt to use one of many various Centralized Exchanges, or "CEX" to purchase ETH.

In the link below you will find a list of exchanges.

Some Centralized Exchanges may not be available in your jurisdiction.

<https://coinmarketcap.com/rankings/exchanges/>

+++ 2. Bridging

If you have already native BNB and wish to bridge those assets over to Binance Smart Chain, you can use this bridging tool provided by Binance to transfer, or "bridge" these assets to Binance Smart Chain.

<https://www.bnbchain.org/en/bridge>

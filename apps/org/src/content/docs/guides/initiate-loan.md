## Follow these UI steps to select and execute your liquidity loan:

{% spacer /%}

### Important contract requirements to deploy on Xchange

- Ensure to exclude the X7 Lending Pool Contract from any Max TXs and Max fees.

### Step 1: Navigate to the Initial Liquidity Loans (ILL) deploy page

Click the link here to [Initiate a Liquidity Loan](http://x7finance.org/lending?tab=initiate-loan)

### Step 2: Connect your wallet and select the relevant chain

Choose your desired chain and wallet in the top right hand corner (Ensure your wallet is also connected to the same chain).

![Connect your Cryptocurrency Wallet](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/connect-wallet.png)

### Step 3: Select your loan terms

Select the loan term option that best suits your needs.

![Select the Loan Terms](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/select-iniital-liquidity-loan.png)

Next select the length of the loan and the amount of native token to borrow. The value must be between the value displayed in the notification.

![Select the Loan Amount](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/select-loan-amount.png)

### Step 4: Review Loan terms

Here you will see an overview of the loan details, ensure the loan meets your needs.

![Select the Loan Duration](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/review-loan-terms.png)

### Step 5: Approve Token For Trade

Approve the token for trading on Xchange. Click approve and sign the transaction via your wallet integration provider.

![Select the Loan Duration](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/approve-token.png)

### Step 6: Review Launch Price

This is the most important step: if a market already exists for the pair, you should aim to launch the xchange pair as close to the existing market price as possible. This prevents the newly launched xchange pair from being extremely volatile and ensures tradability at launch.

![Review Launch Price](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/review-launch-price.png)

### Step 7: Deploy the ILL for your project

When all the above steps are completed the 'Inititate Loan' button with become enabled. Click this button and confirm the transaction via your wallet integration provider.

![Select the Loan Duration](https://assets.x7finance.org/images/docs/guides/integrate-ui/supply-liquidity.png)

### Congratulations your ILL is now deployed

Your liquidity pair is now deployed and ready for trading.

You can monitor the repayment progress of your ILL via the [Loan Repayment Page Here](https://www.x7finance.org/lending)

or view all of the loans on any chain using the [Loan Dashboard Page Here](https://www.x7finance.org/lending?tab=all-loans)
